Cancer Information Center

Interactive body map

Educational only — not diagnostic. Highlights show where a documented condition, a patient-reported symptom, a treatment target, or a possible medication-related effect may be relevant. A medicine name alone never implies organ damage.

Click a highlighted region to see why it is shown, in plain language.

Medication dashboard

CancerCare explains a clinician-recorded prescription — its documented purpose, common side effects and monitoring. It never selects, starts, stops or changes cancer treatment, and never turns a document into a new prescription. Always confirm with your oncology team.
